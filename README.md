**Wild Climb**
Wild Climb is a Minecraft mod that adds a climbing system in the style of *The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild*.

**Main Features**
1. Simply press jump while against a wall to climb it, and you can also jump-climb like in the original game.
2. More convenient paraglider usage: as long as there is a paraglider in your inventory, you can press jump in the air to deploy it.
3. Quick wall switching: just move at a corner.

**Mod Dependencies**
[Paragliders](https://github.com/Tictim/Paraglider)
[Player Animation Library](https://github.com/PlayerAnimationLibrary/PlayerAnimationLibrary)
